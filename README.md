## The Last Vessel

*Fragments of a Life Between Memory, Consciousness, and Eternity*

**Writer:** Tishri Clarin  
**Genre:** Philosophical science fiction / autofiction  
**Edition:** Illustrated first edition · 48 pages

### Read the book

**[Read the complete manuscript (Markdown)](THE_LAST_VESSEL/The_Last_Vessel_Manuscript.md)**

*Illustrated PDF, page-image archive, editable Word file, and book poster: pending binary upload.*

A first-person narrative about fragmented memories, recurring dreams, imagined future civilizations, machine consciousness, and humanity's final departure from Earth. It blends personal reflection with speculative storytelling. Its visions and spiritual imagery are presented as elements of the narrator's subjective experience, not verified events.

### Parts and chapters

- **Prologue:** The Man Who Remembered Tomorrow
- **Part I — The Erasure:** Ch. 1 The Wilderness · Ch. 2 The Empty Years
- **Part II — The Perfect World:** Ch. 3 The World at Its Peak · Ch. 4 The Children of Machines
- **Part III — The Last Departure:** Ch. 5 The Dying Sun · Ch. 6 The Last Fleet
- **Part IV — The Doctor of Machines:** Ch. 7 The White Coat · Ch. 8 The Pattern
- **Part V — The Second Self:** Ch. 9 The Voice · Ch. 10 Two in One
- **Part VI — The Poison and the Curse:** Ch. 11 The Poison · Ch. 12 The House
- **Part VII — November: The Unfinished Mission:** Ch. 13 The Expected Arrival · Ch. 14 The Unfinished Mission
- **Epilogue:** What Remains
